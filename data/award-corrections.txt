# Hand corrections to the award winner data. Re-applied on every build,
# so a fix made here survives the yearly refresh.
#
#   remove | <award title> | <Wikidata QID of the work> | <year, or * for any year>
#   add    | <award title> | <Wikidata QID of the work> | <year of the ceremony>
#
# Award titles are as in data/award-lists.txt. A work's QID is on its Wikidata
# page (e.g. Titanic is Q44578). Lines starting with # are ignored.
