How to check an entry

  • Winner list opens the award's Wikipedia article. Winners are the highlighted rows under each year.
  • Wikidata opens the film or series record used for title, director and year matching.
  • Years are the year of the ceremony. A film from 1997 that won in early 1998 is listed as 1998.
  • Found a mistake? It can be fixed in award-corrections.txt, or report it. The full list is also in awards.csv.
